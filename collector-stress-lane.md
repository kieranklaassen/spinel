## What this changes

An object the roots lost is freed by the first collection that runs while it is lost, and the program usually answers right anyway: nothing collected in that window, or the freed slot still held its bytes, or the slot had been handed out again and the lost reference named a live object, which `SPINEL_GC_VERIFY` accepts. `SPINEL_GC_STRESS=1` leaves all three standing. It collects only once 2 KB of objects are live or 2 KB of young strings were made, and 300 of 497 corpus tests (every 11th) run under it without one collection. And where it does collect, with a `C` that keeps `v` and `v + 1`,

```ruby
live = []
300.times { |i| live << C.new(i) << C.new(-i) }
puts live.sum { |o| o.a + o.b }
```

prints 179856 instead of 600, with either verifier on and nothing on stderr.

`SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2` pins both thresholds at 0, so every allocation after the first collects. What a sweep or `sp_slab_free` releases is filled with `0xdb` and kept out of reuse and out of `sp_slab_is_live`, by its pin bit and a bitmap beside it (`sp_slab_quarantine`), 1 MB at a time. The verifier's registry check runs on every mark; its walks of the whole heap run only with `SPINEL_GC_VERIFY=1` beside it, since a collection here is per allocation. The loop above then stops with

```
*** SPINEL_GC_STRESS: the mark reached a freed slot ***
  obj = 0x7f964c024030   phase = remembered   ctx = 0x7f964c020030
  slab: chunk wid=0 cls=3 (80 B) in_use=1 slot=0 epoch=3 ... pin=1
  freed: scan=(nil) size=72
  holder: scan=0x5560828acb80 size=208 old=1
```

and a program that reads a lost object prints `0xdb` where the answer was (`[-2604246222170760229, -2604246222170760229]` for `[7, 8]`, `"\xDB\xDB..."` for a String), or faults on a pointer of it. `docs/internals/gc.md` has the section, "A lost object, made visible".

Deleting one root registration at a time from the emitted C of 161 tests (1,525 deletions) on 422e1ced: a plain run notices 0, level 1 notices 19, level 1 with `SPINEL_GC_VERIFY` 32, with `SPINEL_GC_VERIFY_GEN` 19, and level 2 161, every one of the others' among them. Of the 497 tests, level 1 under the verifier fails 1 and level 2 fails 16 (one more, `gc_locality_build`, outlasts 60 s under both). Of the 71 `GC_MINOR_TESTS`, 3 and 13; those 3 abort under `SPINEL_GC_STRESS=1 SPINEL_GC_VERIFY=1` on the untouched runtime too. Of those 29, 27 build without threads or extra libraries, and AddressSanitizer on a runtime with the slab off reports a heap-use-after-free in 15 of them; that build recycles pooled headers without calling `free`, so it cannot see the rest.

Over the whole corpus on 422e1ced (the 5,444 tests that pass a plain run, 60 s each), level 1 under the verifier fails 24 and level 2 fails 252, the 24 among them. Left out of both counts, because they are not lost objects: 14 that outlast 60 s at level 2 (8 of them at level 1 too), three that print the collector's own thresholds (`gc_first_thread_threshold`, `gc_threshold_no_runaway`, `i1021`), and the two `stack_overflow_*` tests, whose handler the verifier's fault reporter replaces at either level. Of the 252, the mark reaches a freed slot in 112, 69 print `0xdb` where the answer was, 35 give another wrong result, 23 fault on a poisoned pointer, 8 stop on the older verifier report, and in 5 the mark faults on poison the program copied into a live object.

None of them is fixed here. `gc-stress-test` runs a C host that loses an object and a string on purpose, which must read back `0xdb` and stop in the root phase, and five programs that must answer the same under the level, alone and beside the verifier.
