The inspect of a String Range keeps its begin across the end's inspect

Under SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2 `p ("aa".."ac")` printed four poisoned bytes
where "aa" belongs. sp_srange_inspect builds the begin's inspect, then
the end's, and nothing held the first String while the second was
allocated. It is rooted now.

test/str_range_inspect_root.rb joins GC_STRESS_TESTS.
