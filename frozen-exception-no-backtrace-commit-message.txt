A frozen exception takes no backtrace when it is rescued

    e = IOError.new("ab").freeze
    begin
      raise e
    rescue => r
      p r.backtrace
    end

printed [] where CRuby prints nil, and `e.backtrace` after it was []
too.

A rescue gives the exception it takes the frames of the raise as its
backtrace, unless it has one already. CRuby does that at the raise and
leaves a frozen exception as it is, so an error kept as a frozen
constant and raised many times answers nil every time. The rescue now
leaves a frozen exception alone as well. One that had a backtrace
attached before it was frozen keeps that one, as before.
