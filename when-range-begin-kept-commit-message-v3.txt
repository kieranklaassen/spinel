The begin of a String Range written in a when is kept while its end is made

    $x1 = 1.to_s
    n = 0
    1000000.times do |i|
      s = (i + 1).to_s
      case s
      when i.to_s..(i + 3).to_s then n += 1
      end
    end
    p n   # 999981, CRuby 999982

A when whose Range is written on the spot compares the subject with the
two ends, each bound to a temp. The begin was held by that temp alone
while the end was made, so an end that allocates could free it: once in
the million turns above, and at every turn under SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2.
The begin's temp is rooted now when both ends may allocate
(operand_may_allocate). A begin that is a literal without a NUL or a
plain local read, or an end that allocates nothing, compiles to the C
it did.
