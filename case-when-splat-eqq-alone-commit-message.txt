A Class, a Range or a Regexp in `when *list` is asked === alone

sp_case_splat_match took an element equal to the subject for a match
before it asked the element ===. For a Class, a Range and a Regexp the
two differ: `Integer === Integer`, `(1..3) === (1..3)` and `/a/ === /a/`
are false.

    classes = [Integer, :z]
    puts(case Integer when *classes then "in" else "out" end)
    # CRuby: out   Spinel: in

Only a subject of one of those kinds is equal to such an element, so
for such a subject the elements are asked through sp_poly_case_eq
alone; the test is made once a `when`, not once an element. Any other
subject matches an element equal to it, as before.

Test: test/case_when_splat_eqq_alone.rb.
