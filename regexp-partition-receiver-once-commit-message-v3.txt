partition with a Regexp evaluates its receiver once

    $calls = 0
    def line
      $calls += 1
      "row" + $calls.to_s
    end
    p line.partition(/z/)
    p $calls

prints ["row2", "", ""] and 2 where CRuby prints ["row1", "", ""] and
1. (s << "c").partition(/z/) appends twice in the same way.

The emitter builds the Array in the emitted C and wrote the
receiver's C text twice: once for the match, and once more for the
first piece when nothing matches.

The receiver now goes into a C temporary and both places read that.
Nothing else in the emitted C moves: the Array, the match and the
pieces are made in the order they were. The temporary needs no root:
nothing allocates between a match that fails and the copy, and
sp_str_dup roots what it copies.

"key=value".partition(/=/) goes from 909 to 910 instructions a call
and a subject with no match from 911 to 912 (callgrind on
70cddab37194, gcc, 100,000 calls).

Test: test/regexp_partition_receiver_once.rb. Without the change it
prints 9 wrong lines.
