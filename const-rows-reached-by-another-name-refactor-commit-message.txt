rows_value_dropped finds a method's calls through the name index

Whether a method's value is thrown away is asked of every call of its
name, and the walk visited every CallNode of the program for each
such method, in every round of inference that asks whether a
constant's rows are only read by an index. an_calls_named_first hands
out the calls of one name. No generated C changes; compiling optcarrot
takes 11 million instructions fewer of 8,352 million.
