sn_guard_over_hoists writes the jump of a `&.` guard

The statements a `&.` call's typed arm writes when its value hoists
(the saved root count, the jump over the hoisted statements, its
label) move into a function of their own. No generated C changes; the
next commit calls it from the boxed arm.
