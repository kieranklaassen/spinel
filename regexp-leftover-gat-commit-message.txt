`s[re, n] = v` replaces the tenth group and beyond

An index assignment by a Regexp and a group number refused every group
past the ninth:

    w = +"abcdefghijklm"
    w[/(a)(b)(c)(d)(e)(f)(g)(h)(i)(j)(k)(l)/, 10] = "X"
    p w

raised IndexError "index 10 out of regexp"; CRuby prints
"abcdefghiXklm".

The assignment replaces a group by its span, and the spans of the
groups up to the fifteenth are kept, so the arm that names its group at
run time now takes them, with the tests it makes of any group: past the
pattern's groups is "out of regexp", and a group that took no part is
"not matched". A group past the fifteenth is refused as before.

Test: test/string_regexp_group_assign_tenth.rb.
