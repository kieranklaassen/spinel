printed 7; it is 0. With two or more arguments emit_str_mutator_call
appends one argument at a time into a C temporary, and nothing held
the String so far while the next argument was evaluated: a collection
that fell there freed it, and the next append read freed bytes. At
SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2 `s.append_as_bytes("ab", "\xff".b)` stopped in the
collector's check. The temporary is rooted after the first append when
another argument follows; one argument keeps its C.
