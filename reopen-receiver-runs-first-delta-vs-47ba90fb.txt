===== 4. the two probes (the delta comment's section 3, as written) =====
**3. The two probes.**

*A receiver or operand held in a temp ahead of arguments that change it in place.* That is the block itself, and groups f, d and h are that probe: every program whose `R` answers `$s` (or may, by a route the program holds) keeps the tip's C, so nothing is held. For the programs that ARE held (groups p and q) the question turns round: is the held String new? The `--share-strings` cell answers it, since there `$s << "c"` changes the one String in place: all 37 are right there on the head (and wrong on the bare tip, which reads `$s` after `add` ran). Each `R` of those was written to be the hard case for its builtin (`strip` with nothing to strip, `chomp` with no newline, `delete_prefix("zz")` that does not match, `center(1)` that pads nothing, `tr("z", "y")` that changes nothing, `$s * 1`, `$s + ""`), where a runtime could answer the receiver itself. The receiver kinds of the earlier hand-over (one program a kind, 18 programs) ran again: the nine kinds held first (Float, Symbol, Range, Array, Hash, an interpolation, `$s.upcase`, an Integer's `to_s`, a method that ends in an interpolation) differ in C and are right with gcc and clang where the bare tip is wrong with gcc; the three listed but unchanged (Integer, object, `true`) and the six left as master's (a global, a method that answers its parameter, a reader's instance variable, a method with a `return` of the global, a local changed in place, a boxed receiver) have the tip's C. The same cells as under `47ba90fb3da9`.

*A receiver read after arguments a piece newly runs.* This piece moves no argument ahead of a receiver: a call it takes binds the receiver to its temp first and then runs what the arguments hoist, and a call it leaves is the tip's C. What it can change is what a held receiver shows after the arguments ran, which is the first probe. The 23 doors of the earlier hand-over (an argument that can run the program's code by a door the whitelist must not admit) ran again: no cell differs between the bare tip and the head in any of them; 17 have the tip's C and the six admitted ones (d8, d8b, d9, e4, e5, d12) differ in C with the same answers on both, as before. The loss programs of the earlier rounds ran again: 20 programs (the reader's six and u1b, the Door program, the moved-buffer program and the rest). 18 have the tip's C and so the tip's cells. Two differ in C: w3, right on both, and the small Door program, right here in the four cells where the bare tip prints a garbage size with gcc at stress 2 and faults with clang at stress 2.
