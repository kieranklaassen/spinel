`s[re, n] = v` counts a negative group back from the last one

An index assignment by a Regexp and a group number refused every
negative number:

    s = +"hello"
    s[/(e)(l)(l)/, -1] = "X"
    p s

raised IndexError "index -1 out of regexp"; CRuby prints "helXo". A
negative number counts back from the last group there, and only one
past the first group is out of the regexp.

A number that is not a literal from 0 to 9 now names its group at run
time. The group it comes to is tested like any other: one that took no
part raises "regexp group 1 not matched" by the number it came to, as
in CRuby, and a frozen receiver raises FrozenError only where CRuby
gets as far. A literal from 0 to 9 is emitted as before, byte for byte,
and so is the arm that tests no group because its value runs code of its
own first: counted back there, a group that took no part would be
spliced in at no position.

Test: test/string_regexp_group_assign_negative.rb.
