`when Integer` takes a Bignum in a boxed subject

`when Integer` and `in Integer` beside a boxed subject tested the tag
of an Integer that fits and no other. A Bignum in the box is an Integer
under a tag of its own, so it fell to the next arm or to else, silently:

    v = [2**70, :a][0]
    case v
    when Integer then p :integer
    else p :other              # this one ran
    end

`v.is_a?(Integer)`, `Integer === v` and `when Numeric` already took it.
The arm tests both tags, as is_a? does. Its body reads the subject
boxed, so a Bignum goes on through the boxed operators.
