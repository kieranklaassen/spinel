An ensure body's exception stays with its fiber

    f = Fiber.new do
      begin
        raise "in the fiber"
      ensure
        Fiber.yield 1
      end
    end
    f.resume
    begin
      raise "main"
    rescue => e
      p e.cause
    end

printed `#<RuntimeError: in the fiber>`. CRuby prints nil: the fiber is
suspended in its ensure body, and main is handling nothing. The other
way round, a fiber resumed from an ensure body of main raised with
main's exception as its cause, and the body lost its own exception to
any raise made while its fiber was away.

sp_inflight_cause, the exception an ensure body runs for, is one
variable for all fibers. The per-fiber context carried the exception
being handled and the pending cause across a switch, and not this one.
It is now saved and loaded with them, and marked while its fiber is
suspended.
