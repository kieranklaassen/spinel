A pattern binding that would copy its subject onto a String handle is refused

`case line in String => s` binds s to the subject itself. A local that
holds a String handle took a fresh handle over the subject's text where
the subject had none to give (emit_pattern_bind_handle). Without
--share-strings nothing joins the local with the subject, and a local
that holds a reader's String took a copy:

    s = page.text << "a"
    case line
    in String => s
      s << "!"
    end
    p line        # "zz!" in CRuby, "zz" compiled

Under the flag the share facts join the two, so a variable or an element
has the handle; a reader call as the subject (`case page.text`) is not
followed and was copied there as well. A local that is appended to in
place and holds no shared handle did not build without the flag: the
subject's `const char *` went into its `sp_String *` slot.

The binding is refused by name where the subject names a String someone
else holds: a variable, a reader's field, an Array's or a Hash's
element. The refusal goes through share_route_defer: without the flag it
stands, and under the flag the facts decide, so only the subject they
cannot hand over is refused. A variable that holds the handle still
hands it over, and any other subject is still a new String. Two of the
reject tests compile under the flag and are in test/share/reject.list;
the third, a reader call as the subject, is refused in both modes.
