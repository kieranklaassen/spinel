A. WHY IT IS A NEW PIECE.
   Master's own ensure commits changed the ground under the old one. The rescue clauses of a begin with an ensure now run in a frame of their own, and an exception no clause takes is held there as its object. At the ensure's entry master makes the exception's object when the raise carried none, for `$!` in the body and for the cause of a raise in the body, and keeps it alive while the body runs (it is pushed on the `$!` stack, which the collector marks, and popped by a cleanup when the body is left). So the object the old root wrapper rooted is already alive, and what is left of the old fault is the RAW MESSAGE: the class and message in the C locals are what master still raises again after the body, and the message is freed as before.
   By the builder rule the piece is cut down to that remainder, standing on master's rule: the ensure holds the object master makes and raises that.
