A Hash walk whose block deletes the current entry goes on from the next

    h = {"a" => 1, "b" => 2, "c" => 3}
    h.each { |k, _v| h.delete(k) }
    p h

printed {"b" => 2} where CRuby prints {}. Deleting the entry the walk is
at slides the next one into its slot, and the walk stepped past it.
each and each_pair already stay on the slot for Integer and Symbol keys;
String keys, boxed keys, and each_key and each_value with any key did
not.

They keep the key of the turn in a temporary now, and on a turn that
left the Hash shorter they step only if the slot still holds that key.
A turn that deleted nothing steps as before, so a walk that never
deletes pays the copy of the key and one length compare a turn (5 to 7
instructions). Every turn that does not step has made the Hash shorter,
so the walk ends. The Integer and Symbol each is the C it was.

Not changed: map, select and the other walks that build a result; a
walk over a Hash held in a boxed variable; a turn that deletes two
entries at or before the one the walk is at, which skipped two and
skips one; and a turn that deletes an entry and adds one, where CRuby
raises.
