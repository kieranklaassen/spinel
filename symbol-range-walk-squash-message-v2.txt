A Symbol Range walks the names String#upto walks

    p (:y..:ab).to_a    # [] in Ruby; [:y, :z, :aa, :ab]

A Symbol Range literal is walked by name: String#succ from the begin
until the end is met or the name grows past the end's length. That is
the last of String#upto's cases, taken for all of them:

    (:y..:ab)          four names; "y" sorts after "ab": none
    (:Y..:b)           stops at :Z; one character each: the ten between
    (:"10"..:"9")      :"10"; digits go by number: none
    (:"1"..:"010")     999 names; ten
    (:aa..:z)          :aa; the begin is the end's successor: none
    (:"z.0"..:"z.09")  100 names; "z.9" steps past the end: ten

and `include?`, `each` and `map` on such a Range answer from those
names.

Both names are literals, so emit_range_expr asks at compile time
whether the loop is String#upto's walk for them (sym_range_plain_walk)
and keeps the loop where that is proved. Any other Range calls
sp_PolyArray_from_symbol_range, which walks with sp_str_upto_each, the
walk String Ranges use, and interns each name through the unit's own
table. Those are the Ranges the loop walked wrong and four kinds the
test does not prove: two names of digits where the end is not its own
number at the begin's width, a begin shorter than an end whose carry
lands on a 0 that no letter or digit stands before (:"0z", :"a.09"), a
begin one character longer than the end with the shape of its
successor, and two different names where either is not ASCII. Two names
of digits past 18 keep the loop, right or not: the runtime walks by
number only up to 18 digits.

The loop's cursor is now a root. Every name past the first is a String
of its own, made by sp_str_succ, and nothing held it while its Symbol
was made, which allocates when the name is new: `p (:ax..:az).to_a`
aborts under SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2 with gcc and with clang, and so does
test/symbol_range_enum.rb. The first cursor is the begin's own name,
which the collector passes over where it is a literal's.

Cost (callgrind, gcc): the root is 16 to 64 instructions a walk: 19 on
a walk of one name (`(:qa..:qa)`: 202,987,017 to 221,987,021 for
1,000,000 walks) and 64 on one of 26 (`(:qa..:qz)`: 3,209,740,876 to
3,216,168,755 for 100,000). A Range of those four kinds that was right
pays more on a short walk: `(:"002"...:"3")` 732 to 1,535 a walk.

test/symbol_range_walk.rb has each case; 13 of its 28 lines differ
from Ruby on master in a plain run. It and test/symbol_range_enum.rb
are added to GC_STRESS_TESTS.
