A return, next or break out of a rescue modifier's expression pops its frame

    def first_number(words)
      words.each do |w|
        return Integer(w) if w =~ /\A\d+\z/
      end rescue nil
      0
    end

    p first_number(["a", "42", "b"])
    p Integer("oops")

printed 42 and then "*** stack smashing detected ***: terminated"
where CRuby reports the ArgumentError. Called 65 times, first_number
alone stopped the program with a SystemStackError.

`expr rescue fallback` arms a setjmp frame around expr, but the frame
was never counted in g_exc_frame_depth, the number a return, next or
break reads to pop the frames it leaves. Such an exit left the
modifier's frame on the stack, its jmp_buf in a function that had
returned, and the next uncaught raise jumped into it. An ensure inside
the expression miscounted the same way: under an outer ensure it handed
its exception straight to that one, past the modifier that rescues it.

Both forms of the modifier now count their frame while the expression
is emitted. A block's `break` is the one exit that asks whether any
frame stands between it and its wrapper, to choose a goto over a throw.
Past nothing but modifier frames it stays the goto it was: they have no
ensure to run, and the wrapper's landing restores sp_exc_top.
g_exc_modifier_frames records which frames are a modifier's, for that
question alone.
