An ensure runs when no rescue clause of its begin matches

    def go
      raise TypeError, "t"
    rescue ArgumentError
      puts "not reached"
    ensure
      puts "ensure"
    end

never printed "ensure": the exception went on and the ensure body was
skipped, so a lock stayed held and a file stayed open. `exit` inside a begin
with a bare rescue skipped it the same way. A begin with an ensure and no
rescue was right, and so was a clause that matched.

Such a begin has one frame. On landing the clauses run with that frame
popped, and the arm for no match raised again at once: a jump to the
enclosing frame, past the ensure. That arm now stores the exception as the
begin with no rescue does and falls into the ensure, which raises it again
after its body. What the clauses let through still leaves by that raise and
not by the short way to an enclosing ensure, which would pass the rescue
clauses around this begin.

Nothing is added on the path with no exception, and a begin that lacks
either clause emits the C it did.
