A `when` beside a Rational or a Complex compares by value

A Rational literal arm read its subject as an Integer, so it missed a
Rational or a Float that equals it and said nothing:

    case Rational(1, 2)
    when 0.5r then puts "hit"
    else puts "miss"           # Spinel: miss, CRuby: hit
    end

`case 0.5` with `when 0.5r` and `case Complex(0, 2)` with `when 2i`
missed the same way. An arm that is not a literal compared the two
with C's `==`, which a struct does not have, so `when Rational(1, 2)`
beside a Rational did not build.

A Rational beside an Integer, a Float, a Bignum or a Rational, on
either side, and two Complex are now compared as a pinned value is
(emit_pm_eq: boxed, by sp_poly_eq). The literal arms keep their own
test beside an Integer subject, where it was right.

A Complex beside a real number is left as it was and still does not
build (`case Complex(2, 0)` with `when 2`): boxed, the two do not
compare equal, and in CRuby they do.
