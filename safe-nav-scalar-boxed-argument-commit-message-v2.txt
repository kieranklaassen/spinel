A `&.` call to between? or a Float's clamp takes a boxed bound

    def id(x) = x
    id(:s)
    def flt(v) = v ? 1.5 : nil
    f = flt(true)
    p f&.clamp(1.0, id(1.2))    # 1.2

This raises NoMethodError ("undefined method 'clamp' for an instance of
Float"), and `i&.between?(id(1), id(20))`, on an Integer or a Float,
does not build. Both were right before "v&.fdiv(2) and the other
Ruby-defined Integer methods keep the &.": that left every `&.` call on
the typed emitter, which has no arm for these two with a boxed bound.

Those two are moved onto their builtins/ definition after all, as
`(t = recv; t.nil? ? nil : copy(t, args))`, the shape the Enumerable
move gives a `&.` call. Every other `&.` call stays on the typed
emitter, a Bignum receiver among them.
