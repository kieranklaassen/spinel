Move the copy of one module method out of process_include_body

process_include_body copies a module's method into the includer in the
tail of its loop, under the name the loop chose for it. A method whose
super goes on to another method of the module has to be copied with
that one, each under a name of its own, so the copy becomes
include_copy_scope(c, ci, ms, dst_name) and the loop calls it.

The lines are moved as they stand. No generated C changes
(tools/cident.sh: 0 differ).
