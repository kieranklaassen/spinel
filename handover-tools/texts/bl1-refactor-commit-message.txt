Refactor: the constant rewrite takes the read, not the call

rewrite_const_alias_kind_arg took an is_a? call and checked its name and
argument count before the guards. The guards speak of a constant read
alone, so the function becomes rewrite_const_alias_read and takes the
read; the caller keeps the test of the call. The scan for a program's
own is_a?, kind_of? or instance_of? becomes program_names_method with
the list of names as an argument.

No change in the C of any program.
