String#delete_prefix and #delete_prefix! are byte-exact over a NUL

Both measured the receiver and the prefix with strlen, where
delete_suffix reads the byte length. A receiver carrying a NUL lost its
tail whether the prefix matched or not ("xa\0b".delete_prefix("x") was
"a", and so was "a\0b".delete_prefix("q")), and a prefix carrying one
matched by its bytes before the NUL ("abc".delete_prefix("a\0zzz") was
"bc"). On such a receiver delete_prefix! with a prefix that was not
there answered the receiver where it is nil. Both read the byte length
now, as the limitations section already says they do.
