The begin of a String Range written in a when is kept while its end is made

    case s
    when i.to_s..(i + 3).to_s then n += 1
    end

A when whose Range is written on the spot compares the subject with the
two ends, each bound to a temp. The begin was held by that temp alone
while the end was made, so an end that allocates could free it: the arm
above matches 194 times in 200 turns, and matched 0 times under
SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2. The begin's temp is rooted now when both ends may
allocate. A begin that is a literal or a plain local read, or an end
that allocates nothing, compiles to the C it did.
