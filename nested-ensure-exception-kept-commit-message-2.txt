An exception handed from an inner ensure to the outer one stays alive

    def nested(n)
      begin
        begin
          raise ArgumentError, "the message of error number " + n.to_s
        ensure
          $inner += 1
        end
      ensure
        churn
      end
    end

handed its caller an error whose message was another String of the
program, in a plain run, where `churn` only allocates.

The inner region hands the exception to the outer one by popping the
outer region's frame and jumping to its ensure body. The collector keeps
what the exception slots hold up to sp_exc_top, and the slot the message
was read from lies above that once the frame is popped: the first
collection in the outer ensure body freed it. The hand-on now puts the
message and the object in the popped frame's slot, as a landing in that
frame would have left them.
