The format piece's commit message, v8 to v9: every changed line, OLD and NEW.

OLD:
interpolation counts as one only where it is provably a new String:
a literal part that is not empty stands among its parts, or its one
part is embedded (`"#{s}"`). CRuby answers the embedded String
itself for one whose other parts are all empty (`"#{s}#{""}"`), and
such a format keeps the C it had.

NEW:
interpolation counts as one only where it has a part to evaluate and
is provably a new String: a literal part that is not empty stands
among its parts, or its one part is embedded (`"#{s}"`). CRuby
answers the embedded String itself for one whose other parts are all
empty (`"#{s}#{""}"`), and such a format keeps the C it had.
