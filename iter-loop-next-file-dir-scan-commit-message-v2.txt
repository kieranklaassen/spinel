A next in a File, ARGF or Dir loop, product or scan ends its own turn

    n = 0
    begin
      File.open("log.txt").each_line { |l| next if l.size > 3; n += 1 }
      raise "late"
    rescue => e
      puts e.message
    end

ends with an uncaught "late"; CRuby prints late. The next popped the
begin's rescue frame. Under an ensure the same next ended the whole
loop and went to the ensure, and in a proc it returned from the proc.

Each of these runs its block inside a C loop its emitter writes, and
the emitter did not record that loop, so a next there was emitted for
whatever stood around the call: a File's each_line, each, each_char,
each_byte and each_codepoint, ARGF.each_line, a Dir's each, each_child
and each_entry; product with a block; scan with a block, read as a
value or with groups in its Regexp; and, read as a value,
each.with_index with a block and each.with_index followed by each.

The File, ARGF and Dir loops and the scan now run the block through
emit_iter_loop_stmts, as a String's each_line does. That call also
makes the block's own locals new in every turn, which these loops did
not (File#each_line { |l| y ||= l; print y } printed the first line
in every turn), and places the label a redo needs, which they refused.
emit_iter_step_body records the loop for product and each.with_index
(c_loop_enter), and each.with_index followed by each records it around
its walk of the body. The record is made for the blocks the String
iterators record: one with a next of its own and no next, break or
redo under a rescue modifier.

A redo in a block with such a jump under a rescue modifier stays
refused in the File, ARGF and Dir loops and the scan: its goto would
leave the modifier's frame on the stack, as it does in a String's
loop, so these loops place no label for that block.

Not changed: the loops that take the block's value as their answer
(find with a fallback proc, an Enumerator's find, detect and
take_while, a boxed Array's index and rindex, each.with_index followed
by reduce, take_while.with_index, and slice_when, chunk and
chunk_while followed by to_a) have the same next, and it cannot be
cured there alone: they also drop the value a next carries.

Test: test/loop_block_next_own_turn.rb. Fifteen such loops, each with
a next under a begin, under an ensure, in a proc, beside an ensure
inside the block, and under an ensure in a begin/rescue inside the
block (six of them for seventy turns); ARGF.each_line under a begin;
a break under a begin where it was right before, and thirteen blocks
with a next under a rescue modifier, which stay right; the File, Dir
and scan loops also with a local first set inside the block. Without
the change the File loops print their first line in every turn and
the first raise ends the program.
test/loop_block_redo_runs_turn_again.rb: a redo in the File and Dir
loops and the scan, with a local of the block and under a begin.
Without the change it does not build.
