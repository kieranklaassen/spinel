Two helpers store and raise again what an ensure region waits with

The store of the landed frame's exception into an ensure region's
locals was written three times: in emit_begin, in
emit_call_synchronize_arms and in emit_array_filter_loop.
emit_ensure_exc_store writes it. The raise of that exception once the
ensure body is done was written five times: twice in emit_begin, in
those two and in emit_ensure_exc_block_out. emit_ensure_exc_raise
writes it. The C is unchanged.
