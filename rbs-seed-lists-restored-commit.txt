rbs-seed-test runs its checks again

#7524 left one line in the Makefile where the two lists stood:

    RBS_SEED_CHECKS :=  \

The backslash joins the next two lines to it, so the definition of
RBS_SEED_RESULTS became the value of RBS_SEED_CHECKS. RBS_SEED_RESULTS
was never defined and RBS_SEED_RUN_CHECKS was defined nowhere.
rbs-seed-test had no prerequisites, its recipe looped over no result
file, and it printed "rbs-seed-test: pass".

Both lists are back as they stood before #7524: 54 names in
RBS_SEED_CHECKS and 26 in RBS_SEED_RUN_CHECKS. array_transpose_nil
joins RBS_SEED_RUN_CHECKS. #7524 added its .rb, its .expected and its
sig file, which is all the .run rule reads, and no arm in the .res
rule's case, which every RBS_SEED_CHECKS name needs.

Before, make rbs-seed-test printed pass with no file under
build/rbs-seed-results. Now it builds 81 (54 .res, 27 .run), each
holds 1, and it prints pass (linux-x86_64, gcc 13.3.0).
