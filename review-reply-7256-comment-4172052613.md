Fixed in 90e84693, by the key and not by a reset.

The marks are now kept by the table, its count and its version, as `an_calls_named_first` (src/analyze_util.c) and `singleton_def_of` (src/codegen_call.c) keep theirs. A reset at the entry of `codegen_program` would add a line to a function of 1,985 lines, and CONTRIBUTING.md does not let a function over 1,000 lines grow.

The case that is left, a freed table's address reused by a table of equal count and version, needs `codegen_program` to be given a second table in the process. It never is: `main` loads one (`nt_load_text`, src/main.c:798) and calls `codegen_program` once (src/main.c:816), with no loop around them, and that is the only call and the only `main` in src/ (`bin/spinel` and the scale test's `build/spinel-work` are both it). The one other table a process can load, the temporary one a static eval string is parsed into (`sce_graft`, src/analyze_desugar.c), is imported into the program's table and freed; `codegen_program` is never given it. So no program was compiled wrongly before the change, and none is compiled differently after it: the C of the 5,712 programs in test/, benchmark/ and the package tests, and of optcarrot, is the same with and without 90e84693.
