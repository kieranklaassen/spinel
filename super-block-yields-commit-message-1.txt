yield_target_push: the yield-target entry is pushed by one function

emit_inline_call_x records a literal block as yielding to the block
current at its call site, with the context that block runs under. The
push, its pop and the test for a forwarded block that has an entry move
out into yield_target_push, yield_target_pop and
yield_target_is_current, so the two other inliners that make a literal
block current (a `super`, a constructor whose initialize yields) can
record theirs the same way. No change in the emitted C.
