One helper hands a waiting exception to the enclosing ensure

An ensure region whose body is done hands the exception it waits with to
the ensure region around it, and the line that does it was written out
three times: in emit_begin, in the region of Mutex#synchronize and in
the loop of select! and its kin. emit_ensure_exc_hand_on emits it for
all three. The C is unchanged.
