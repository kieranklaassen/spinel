A pinned Rational or Complex is compared by value

`in ^y` beside a Rational or a Complex compared the two with C's `==`,
which a struct does not have, so the program did not build:

    x = Rational(1, 2)
    y = Rational(1, 2)
    case x
    in ^y then puts "hit"      # error: invalid operands to binary ==
    else puts "miss"
    end

emit_pm_eq already compares an Array, a Hash and a Bignum subject
boxed, by sp_poly_eq, with the value rooted. A Rational beside an
Integer, a Float, a Bignum or a Rational, on either side, and two
Complex now go the same way.

A Complex beside a real number is left as it was and still does not
build (`case Complex(2, 0)` with `in ^n`, n = 2): boxed, the two do
not compare equal, and in CRuby they do.
