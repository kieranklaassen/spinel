any?, all?, none? and one? given a Regexp stop where CRuby stops

Each of the four asks the pattern of an element at a time and stops
where the answer is known: any? and none? at the first match, all? at
the first miss, one? at the second match. Spinel asked every element
and counted, so $~ was left at the last element's:

    a = ["xb", "q", "yb"]
    p a.any?(/(.)b/), $1
    p a.all?(/(.)b/), $~

printed true, "y" and false, #<MatchData "yb" 1:"y">; CRuby prints
true, "x" and false, nil.

The loop now stops at those points. The answers do not change.

It stops where a method's registers are provably its own
(match_frame_closed) and where the program never reads them. A program
that reads them and fails that test keeps the count: there a method
whose only match is the quantifier saves no frame, so its caller reads
what it leaves, and a caller that read nil after a last miss would read
the first match instead.

Tests: test/quantifier_regexp_last_match.rb. The two
test/quantifier_regexp_count_kept_*.rb are programs that keep the
count.
