An exception keeps its cause through an ensure

    def go
      begin
        raise ArgumentError, "first"
      rescue ArgumentError
        raise TypeError, "second"
      end
    end

    def mid
      go
    ensure
      $n = 1
    end

    begin
      mid
    rescue => g
      p g.cause
    end

printed nil. CRuby prints #<ArgumentError: first>.

An ensure holds the exception in its region's locals while its body runs
and raises it again after, and that raise decided the cause anew, from
what was being handled there. The cause now waits with the exception, in
a local each of the three frames declares: it is a root while the ensure
body runs, it is handed to an outer ensure with the rest (through
sp_pending_cause, which the collector marks, until that region roots
it), and `sp_exc_pass_cause` gives it to the raise. An object that
carries a cause of its own keeps it.

Left alone: a rescue that does not match still loses the cause.
