WHAT IT CURES. A rescue none of whose clauses takes an exception raised it again as a NEW raise, so the cause
was worked out anew: the handled exception, whatever the exception was raised with (an explicit nil, another
exception). The fault is old for a raise in the begin's own body. Since the tip raises a nested ensure's
exception into the rescue around it, every exception leaving an inner ensure for clauses that decline comes down
this road: the cause tests of the two rows closed as done by master were right on every earlier bare tip and are
wrong on this one. This is the cure of that (the side find named C1 before).

THE CUT. On upstream's own mechanism only: the three pass-throughs (emit_rescue's, and the rescue modifier's as a
statement and as a value) set sp_reraise_continues, and sp_raise_cls, continuing with no object, keeps the cause
still staged. No field, no test of a clause's class.

THE HOLE, AND WHY IT IS LEFT. A clause whose operand runs code of the program (rescue pick, a method call) may
raise and rescue on its own between the landing and the pass-through, which replaces the staged cause. Holding
the staged cause across the clauses in a rooted local cured that, and was dropped for two reasons. It is not
free: an address-taken local makes gcc put its stack protector into a function that had none, 5 instructions a
call on a path nothing is raised on (clang none). And every program that shows the hole also walks a fault of
master's (the last side find below), so a kept test of it would read a freed message under SPINEL_GC_STRESS=2.
So a rescue one of whose clauses has such an operand keeps master's pass-through line byte for byte
(rescue_operand_runs_code: anything but a read of a constant or of a variable, splatted or not). The "Not here"
program of the body prints "#<KeyError: low>" on the bare tip and on the head in six cells, where CRuby prints
nil; and the program with no explicit cause, which the bare tip has right, stays right in six cells. Clauses on a
list held in a constant and on a class held in a local are cured, and are in the kept test.
