emit_scope_decls_ends roots a parameter through emit_param_root

The switch that roots a parameter by the form its type takes (a boxed
value, a String, a String Range, any other pointer) moves out of
emit_scope_decls_ends into emit_param_root, called from where it
stood. No generated C changes.
