An exception that waits for an ensure keeps its frames

In a --debug build

    def outer(x)
      mid(x)
    rescue ArgumentError
      nil
    ensure
      @seen = true
    end

cut the backtrace of a TypeError raised under `mid` to `outer` and up.
The arm for no match sets sp_bt_keep before it raises again; the raise
after the ensure body did not, and took a new snapshot at the begin.

A debug build now saves the frames when no clause matches and puts them
back for the raise after the ensure body, so they survive a body that
raises and rescues an exception of its own, which takes the one buffer.
A build without --debug emits the C it did.
