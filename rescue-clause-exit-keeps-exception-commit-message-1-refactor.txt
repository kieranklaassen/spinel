One emitter writes the frame pops and rescue-body pops from their counts

emit_frame_unwind found its two counts and wrote them out in one body, so
a caller that counts the rescue bodies another way had to repeat the
emission. The counts are now arguments of emit_unwind, and
emit_frame_unwind and emit_cur_exc_restore find theirs as before and call
it. No generated C changes.
