An ensure runs when a rescue clause of its begin raises

    def go
      raise ArgumentError, "y"
    rescue ArgumentError
      puts "logged"
      raise
    ensure
      puts "ensure"
    end

printed "logged" and never "ensure". A new `raise`, a call that raises, an
`exit` and a `throw` in the clause skipped the ensure body the same way.

The begin's one frame is popped when it lands, so the clauses ran with no
frame of this begin, and what they raised went to the enclosing one. A
clause's body now runs under a frame of its own when the begin has an
ensure. It is pushed on the landing path, so a begin that raises nothing
enters one frame as before. Its landing stores the exception for the
ensure, as the arm for no match does, and a throw or a proc's return goes
on after the ensure body as it does out of the begin's body. A `retry` pops
the frame before it jumps; `return`, `break` and `next` count it already.
