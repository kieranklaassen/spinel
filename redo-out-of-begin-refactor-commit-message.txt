One helper opens a redo label

The four places that open a redo label (a loop body, the block spliced
at a yield, an iterator's step statements and its loop statements) each
tested the stack for room, took a temp for the label and stored the
body and the label. redo_label_push does that once.

No change in generated C. One case that cannot be seen: with 64 labels
open, emit_loop_body took a temp number before it found the stack
full; now none is taken.
