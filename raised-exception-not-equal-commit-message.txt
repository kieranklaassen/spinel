An exception that was raised is not == to one that never was

    begin
      raise "ab"
    rescue => e
      p e == RuntimeError.new("ab")
    end

printed true where CRuby prints false, and so did
`[e].include?(RuntimeError.new("ab"))`.

Exception#== is class, message and backtrace. sp_exc_eq compared the
first two and took the backtraces for equal. The backtrace is nil until
a rescue takes the exception or #set_backtrace attaches one, so one
that was raised and one that never was now differ, and two that have a
backtrace compare by its lines. In a release build those lines are none
(docs/limitations.md), so two raised exceptions of one class and
message still compare equal wherever they were raised; a --debug build
has their frames and tells them apart as CRuby does.
