A zip or combination block in a method that yields is handed its elements

    def each_pair_of(a, b)
      a.zip(b) { |x, y| yield x, y }
    end
    each_pair_of([1, 2], ["a", "b"]) { |n, s| p [n, s] }

printed [nil, nil] twice where CRuby prints [1, "a"] and [2, "b"], and
`def pairs(a) = a.combination(2) { |x, y| yield x + y }` did not build
for an Integer Array. The same with `&blk` and `blk.call`, with the
yield after the loop, in a class's own `each`, and for `permutation`.

A method that yields is spliced into its caller with its locals renamed
(rename_local), and each is still declared in its scope under the name
it was written with. iter_ewi_zip_poly_arms asked the scope for the
block's parameters by the new name, found neither, and emitted the loop
with no binding at all. iter_combination_cons_arms and
emit_poly_combination_param asked the same way for the parameter's
type, took the miss for a boxed value and wrote one into an Integer
Array's slot.

The four lookups now go through block_param_local, which asks by the
name as written when the new one is not found. Where the first lookup
finds the local, at the top level and in every method that is not
spliced, the C is what it was.

with --enable-frozen-string-literal). master does not build it; section
by section, 17 of its 29 lines are wrong or raise and the other 12 are
in the three sections that do not build.
