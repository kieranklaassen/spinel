The defaults-ahead rule and its temp are split out for a second site

defaults_run_ahead answered which defaults of a call run ahead of it,
from the call's layout, and emit_default_ahead made one of them into
its temp. A bare super fills the parent's parameters by another route
and needs both.

The rule is now defaults_ahead_of, over the default each parameter
takes at the site (none where it is given), and the temp is
emit_value_ahead, over the slot's text and whether the temp takes a
root. defaults_run_ahead and emit_default_ahead hand them what they
computed before.

C: no program of the corpus differs (tools/cident.sh).
