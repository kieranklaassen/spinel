An unboxed String Range's first and last raise RangeError on its open side

`p ("aa"..).last` and `p (.."ad").first` printed nil, where CRuby raises
RangeError (cannot get the last element of endless range, cannot get
the first element of beginless range). #first and #last shared the
template of #begin and #end, which rightly answer nil for the open
side. Their templates now test the bound and raise for an absent one
(sp_srange_open_raise).

A boxed String Range takes sp_poly_first and sp_poly_last and is not
changed here.
