An ensure body runs to its end under a throw, a break or a return

An ensure body is entered with the unwind that reached it still set in
`sp_unwind_kind`: the state is saved in locals, the body runs, the
state is put back and the unwind resumed. Every begin/ensure ends that
way, so one inside the body, its own or one of a method the body
called, found the outer unwind set, took it for its own and resumed it
from there. The rest of the outer body never ran:

    def log(s)
      begin
        print s
      ensure
        print "."
      end
    end

    def called
      catch(:x) do
        begin
          throw :x, 8
        ensure
          log("a")
          log("b")
          print " c "
        end
      end
    end
    p called

printed `a.8` where CRuby prints `a.b. c 8`. The same under a break
out of a block and under a proc's return, with an ensure written inside
the body, through a method's or a block's own ensure, and at any depth.
What the body did after such a call was lost with it: a return, a raise
or a second throw from the ensure never took over.

The body now runs with no unwind in flight: `sp_unwind_kind` is cleared
once it is saved, and the saved state is put back after the body, as
before. A body that runs no code of the program and holds no ensure of
its own (`$n += 1`) has nothing in it that could resume an unwind, and
compiles to the C it did.
