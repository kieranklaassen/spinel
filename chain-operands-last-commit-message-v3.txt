A call that declined its operands once renders them last

    def where(x) = (@w << x; self)
    q = Q.new.where(col(1)).where(col(2))...

took seconds to compile at 16 links and a minute at 20.
emit_operands_in_order renders two or more observable operands, emits
the call with them bound, and declines when the call did not take
them so; the caller renders the whole call again. A method written in
Ruby declines every time, and as the receiver of the next link it is
emitted twice, so each link doubled the work below it. A call that
has declined is now remembered by node and takes the operands_last
path, as a lone operand does: 200 links compile in seconds.

`make scale-test` compiles test/scale/call_chain_operands.rb, forty
links in seven shapes; without the change it is killed at the limit.
